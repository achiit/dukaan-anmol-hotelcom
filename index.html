Our story

We started Anmol Hotel about four years ago with a simple idea: a traveller should find a clean bed, a hot cup of chai and a friendly face, all at one address on Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Marg.

The café began as a few tables and grew into the colourful place you see today, with green counters, a cloud ceiling and blue hanging lights. Many guests who came for a room now come back just for the cold coffee.

Whether it is a family, a business trip or friends meeting up, we want you to feel at home.
